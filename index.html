Get to know Canada!

Founded on July 1, 1867, Canada is the second largest country in the world by area. Canada is one of the few countries in the world with a coastline to three different oceans: Pacific to the West, Atlantic to the East, and Arctic to the North. Between these coastlines, the land holds mountains, foothills, forest, grasslands, and baren cold land up north. The population of Canada small - approx. 40 million - but it is known for the multicultural identity held between people.
